Who We Are

A Dance Group Built on Passion

Founded in August 2024 by Melanie Capps and Dixie Stevens, Sassafras was born from a simple belief: dance belongs to every woman, at every age.

We give women who love to dance and perform a platform to express their passion — and to keep on thriving and jiving no matter where life takes them.
