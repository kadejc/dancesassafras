Become a Sassafras

Join the Group

Think you have what it takes? We'd love to meet you. Fill out the application below and we'll be in touch.

If you're over 45, love to dance and have experience dancing, come join us.

We perform all over the DFW Metroplex at all types of venues. We have 11 dances (jazz, tap, and lyrical) and 5 Christmas dances. We have a wide variety of dancers from ballroom, jazz, tap, and cheerleaders.

We rehearse at 2155 Dance Studio:

  • Tuesday 3:15–4:15 — Lil Sass class (new to Sass dancers should start here)
  • Tuesday 4:30–6:30 — Sassafras rehearsal
  • Saturday 10:00–11:30 — Sassafras rehearsal

All rehearsals are not required. If you cannot make the Tuesday 3:15 class to begin, email director@sassafrastx.com and we can discuss other options.

If you have any questions, email me.
