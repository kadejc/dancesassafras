Our Story

About Sassafras

Who We Are

Born from a Love of Dance

Sassafras Dance Group was founded in August 2024 by Melanie Capps and Dixie Stevens with a mission to inspire joy and passion for movement — and to celebrate dance at every stage of life.

Based in Dallas, TX, Sassafras gives women who love to dance and perform a platform to express their passion and to keep on thriving and jiving, no matter their age.

From high-energy performances to elegant productions, we bring artistry, heart, and a whole lot of fun to every stage we step on.

Our Mission

Dance with Class

We exist to inspire joy and passion for movement — and to prove that dance doesn't have an expiration date. Every woman deserves a stage, and Sassafras is here to make sure she has one.

We're always looking for passionate women to join our family. Whether you're a seasoned performer or someone who just never stopped loving to dance, Sassafras is your home.

Our Values

Artistry

We treat every performance as a work of art — thoughtfully choreographed, beautifully executed.

Community

We support one another on and off the stage. Sassafras is a family first and a dance group second.
