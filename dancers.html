The Team

Our Dancers

Meet the talented women who make Sassafras what it is. Each dancer brings a unique voice, background, and passion to the group.

Melanie Capps
Co-Founder • "Captain"

Melanie has been dancing since age 7 and has never left the studio. A lieutenant of her high school drill team, she went on to dance at Kilgore College and earned a BBA in Accounting from Texas State along with her CPA license. She founded her own dance studio, then taught dance for 13 years at Dance Movement Studio — directing and choreographing hundreds of dances, productions, and competitions. Today she performs annually at Prestonwood Church's Gift of Christmas, teaches Zumba, competes with an adult hip hop team, and performs with the Spectacular Follies twice a year.

Gina Luna
Assistant Director • "Viva'"

Gina has been dancing and performing since her youth — drill team, cheerleading captain, and then a Dallas Cowboys Cheerleader performing at Texas Stadium. She has since danced with Plano Metropolitan Ballet, Gotta Dance, Evoke Dance Team, and The Spectacular Follies. Gina holds a B.S. in Kinesiology from UNT and is a Texas Board Certified EC-12 P.E. and Visual Arts teacher. She has been married to her husband Alan for 30+ years and they have three adult children.

Debbie Carroll-Boyce
"Firecracker"

Debbie began teaching baton twirling and dance while in high school and earned a college scholarship as feature twirler at The University of Texas. She holds degrees in Theatre and Communications along with Master's degrees in Education and Management Administration, and has served as a teacher, counselor, and school principal for Plano and Frisco ISD. Debbie continued her dance studies at SMU and performed with the David Kirby Dance Group and Dallas Ballet Theatre. She is a nationally recognized winner of the Ms. Senior America Contest and is currently a Realtor with Ebby Halliday. Debbie is an original Sassafras member.

Nancy Griggs
"Sugar"

Nancy discovered early that there is great joy in being on stage. Her Colorado background in music theater and modern dance led her to the Follies. Originally from Wichita, Kansas, Nancy is a retired speech pathologist who enjoys tango and ballroom performance. When not dancing, her heart is in flowers — she is a Sogetsu ikebana teacher giving demonstrations and workshops throughout the Dallas area.

Betsy Lee
"Sparkle"

Betsy took her first dance lesson at 43 and hasn't stopped since. She is one of the original founders of USA Dance Dallas, a local chapter of USA Dance, Inc., serving 12 years as Treasurer. Through the years she has learned ballroom, Argentine Tango, West Coast Swing, and Salsa. In 2016 she took on tap with the Dallas Tap Dazzlers and joined the Follies cast in 2017, becoming a showgirl and dance ensemble member. She joined the Follies board as Treasurer in 2022. Betsy is an original Sassafras member.

Erin Meehan
"Sassy"

Erin started dancing at age five in ballet, tap, and jazz, studying with Buster Cooper, Gus Giordano, Lea Darwin, and Doug Caldwell. She also performed in community theater productions including The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas. In 2022 Erin joined the Texas Glammas and performed throughout Ireland, including marching in the St. Patrick's Day Parade in Limerick. She is an original Sassafras member, a grandmother to twin baby girls, and competes in the National Reining Horse Association with her quarter horse.

Liz Osborne
"Gogo"

Liz is a dedicated dancer and choreographer, previously a member of J. David Kirby's Dance Energy and Jazz City Dancers, with stage credits including Oklahoma!, 42nd Street, and The Best Little Whorehouse in Texas. With over 30 years of teaching experience, she serves as Artistic Director of Evoke Dance Company in Plano. She is grateful for the support of her husband of 30 years and their four sons.

Audra Wadsworth
"Sexy"

Audra took dance lessons as a child at Frieda Sexton's dance school in Garland and went on to attend Booker T. Washington High School (The Arts Magnet), majoring in dance. In 2018 she took her first ballroom lesson and was hooked. She continues to study contemporary, lyrical, jazz, and ballroom dance, and is a member of Sassafras and Movement Loft's dance company, performs in the Spectacular Follies, and appears with Mary Ruiz Entertainment.
